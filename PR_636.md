## Summary

Implements abandoned checkout recovery for FacilPay: merchants can opt in to a single recovery email for expired checkout sessions that have a customer email, and customers can reopen the same cart through a fresh session.

Closes #636

## Changes

- Merchant settings: `recoveryEmailsEnabled` (default `false`) and `recoveryEmailDelayMinutes` (default `60`), exposed through `PATCH /v1/merchants/me/settings` with validation and Swagger metadata.
- Checkout sessions: `recoveryEmailSentAt` and `recoveredFromId` columns plus a reversible migration (`1792900000000-AddCheckoutRecoveryEmails.ts`, `up`/`down` verified with a mock QueryRunner).
- `CheckoutRecoveryService`: 10-minute cron that emails eligible expired sessions after the configured delay, claims each session with a conditional update (at most one email per session), honours the `checkout_recovery` notification preference, the global suppression/unsubscribe list and the merchant opt-in.
- Public `POST /v1/checkout/sessions/:publicId/recover` creates a new `OPEN` session pre-filled with the original line items, currency and customer email, linked via `recoveredFromId`.
- Recovery email method, Handlebars template, unsubscribe link and a new `checkout_recovery` preference category; `NotificationsModule` is now imported by `CheckoutSessionsModule`, and `CheckoutSessionsModule` is registered in `AppModule`.
- Analytics: `GET /v1/analytics/checkout-recovery` reports `expiredSessionsWithEmail`, `recoveryEmailsSent`, `recoveredSessions` and `recoveryRate`.
- Docs: `docs/CHECKOUT_SESSIONS.md`, `docs/NOTIFICATIONS.md`, `docs/ENVIRONMENT.md`.
- Test-only fixes in the touched module (stale session fixtures and the expected exception for an expired session), plus a broken relative import in `invoice-line-item.entity.ts` that prevented the checkout test suite from loading.
