## Summary

Merchants can now settle every payment in one preferred Stellar asset (`settlementAsset`), regardless of the asset the payer sent. Non-matching balances are converted at settlement time with a Stellar `pathPaymentStrictSend`, bounded by a per-config `maxSlippagePercent`.

## Changes

- `settlementAsset` (`XLM` or `CODE:ISSUER`) and `maxSlippagePercent` added to `MerchantSettlementConfig` and to `POST /v1/settlements/config` (Swagger decorators included). Payout destinations are validated against the settlement asset code.
- `StellarService.convertAsset()` builds, signs and submits a strict-send path payment, enforces `destMin` on-chain, decodes the executed amount, records the network fee and handles multi-sig/pending signatures.
- Settlement processing groups payments by source Stellar asset (from `metadata.assetCode`/`assetIssuer`, with issuer fallback via platform balances), quotes a path for each non-matching group, rejects quotes whose slippage estimate exceeds the limit, and executes the conversions before committing. An executed amount below `destMin` also fails safely: a `FAILED` settlement with a `failureReason` is recorded and no payments are linked.
- Conversion rate, actual slippage, fee and transaction hash are persisted on the settlement and exposed through `GET /v1/settlements/:id/statement?format=csv|pdf` (new "Asset conversion" section in the PDF) and in settlement events/webhooks.
- Reversible TypeORM migration `1792800000000-AddSettlementAssetConversion`.
- Docs: `docs/SETTLEMENTS.md`, `docs/STELLAR.md`.
